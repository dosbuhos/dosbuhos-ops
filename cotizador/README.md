# Cotizador de eventos

The Acrobat quoter (Cotizador Para Eventos) as a phone app: capture the event, the space and food & beverage; the quote, the per-person price, the minimum-spend check and the internal numbers update as you type; **Propuesta** previews the client view, **PDF** renders the three-page proposal (cover with photo, the evening's rhythm and the space, the investment and next steps) as a real PDF, and **WhatsApp** shares a text summary.

- Worker `cotizador` · static app in `public/` + a small Worker (`src/worker.js`) that gates it with a PIN.
- Secrets (dashboard → cotizador → Settings → Variables and Secrets): `PIN` — the team's PIN. Without it the app is open. `HUB_SECRET` (= the Hub's `SESSION_SECRET`) lets the Hub's signed proxy header in without a PIN. `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (optional) — Claude writes the proposal's opening note from the quote; without it a template note is used. It is read from the account Secrets Store (binding in `wrangler.jsonc`, secret `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in store `1109ee14…`); a plain Worker secret of the same name also works (`AI_MODEL` var overrides the model, default `claude-haiku-5-5`).
- PDF: the app posts the computed quote to `POST /pdf` (form field `q`, JSON); `src/proposal.js` lays it out (Letter, 3 pages, fonts, logos and the photos embedded from `src/assets.js` — generated from `public/fonts`, `public/icons` and `pdfimg/` by `npm run assets` — so the render needs no network) and the Worker prints it with Browser Rendering (binding `BROWSER`, free plan: 10 browser-minutes a day, a few seconds per PDF). Add `?html=1` to preview the template as HTML. Regenerate `src/assets.js` with `npm run assets` after changing fonts or logos.
- Deploys on push through Workers Builds (project `cotizador`, root `cotizador`, deploy `npx wrangler deploy`).
- Prices and rules live in the `P` object at the top of `public/index.html` (menus, bar tiers by hour, minimum spend, space rates and caps, service and IVA). Same logic as the Acrobat form.
- The quote in progress is kept in the browser (localStorage); "Nueva" clears it. No database yet.
- `/personal` — staffing calculator (Personal para eventos): guests + format → slots by role (meseros 1:10–25 by format, bar, líder, cocina per Lalo's counts, lavaloza, estacionamiento), internals vs externals, labor cost at a flat hourly rate, the service-fee split (50/50 piso·cocina, each side pays its own externals, remainder per head) and a requisición text to copy or share by WhatsApp. Rules live in the `R` object at the top of `public/personal.html`. **Open for now** (no PIN, no sign-in) at `cotizador.dos-buhos.workers.dev/personal` so it can be shared while it is tested; it is also registered in the Hub as app `personal` (mode proxy, binding `COTIZADOR`; the Worker serves `personal.html` when `X-DB-App` is `personal`).

- **Personal del evento** (accordion under the quote, always available, not client-facing): the plantilla by the house formula (floor ratios by format, Lalo's kitchen counts, lavaloza, estacionamiento, montaje +2 h), internos vs externos, the night's wages at `R.tarifa`, and the check against the 15 % service fee — each side's half pays its externals first, the remainder is the bono per head, and anything the fee doesn't cover is the house's. The client is charged nothing for staff beyond the 15 %; nothing from this block reaches the proposal, the PDF or the WhatsApp summary. Includes the requisición text (copy / WhatsApp). Rules in the `R` object in `public/index.html`.
- `/guia` — how to fill out the quoter and the rules that don't move, for Luis and Melanie (`public/guia.html`, open).

- **Guardadas** — every quote saves itself to D1 (`quotes`, migration `migrations/0001_quotes.sql`, binding `DB`, database `cotizador`) as soon as it has a client, an event or a guest count: who prepared it (the "Prepara la propuesta" field), client, event, date, guests, total, estado (borrador / enviada / confirmada / cancelada) and the full form state. The top-bar **Guardadas** list searches by client, event, date or author; tap a row to reopen and keep editing (autosaves to the same row), change the estado inline, **Duplicar** to start from a copy, **Eliminar** to delete. **Nueva** starts a fresh row. API: `GET/POST /api/quotes`, `GET/PUT/PATCH/DELETE /api/quotes/:id`, same gate as the app. Migrations are applied with the Cloudflare D1 tool from chat (`d1_migrations` row added by hand); 0001–0002 are applied. Fecha is a date picker (`cv_fecha`, ISO) and the horario two half-hour dropdowns (`cv_inicio`, `cv_fin`, 8:00–2:00); the proposal gets "Sábado 14 de marzo de 2027" and "19:00 – 23:00", the weekday rate (`sp_dia`) follows the date, and "Horas del evento" fills itself from the horario (rounded up). Saved rows keep both `fecha` (text) and `fecha_iso`.

- Space rent (2026-11-10): per person per hour for everyone, never more than the full-space price (which includes `std` hours; extra hours raise the ceiling). The earlier rule charged the full space from 100 guests regardless of hours, so 200 guests × 3 h came out at $125,000 instead of $107,400. Rates in `P.espacios` (from the Nov 9 session; Eric to confirm against what Luis and Melanie were charging).
